SEO Optimization Course

Master the technical and strategic sides of SEO — the skill that keeps businesses visible on Google long after ad budgets run out.

You'll audit real websites, fix technical issues, and build content and link strategies that move rankings, not just theory about them.

What You'll Learn

  • Technical SEO — site audits, crawlability, indexing, and Core Web Vitals.
  • Keyword Research — finding high-intent keywords your competitors are missing.
  • On-Page Optimization — structuring content, metadata, and internal links for maximum visibility.
  • Link Building — earning high-authority backlinks the sustainable way.
  • Local & E-Commerce SEO — ranking for local searches and product pages.
  • SEO Reporting — tracking rankings, traffic, and proving ROI to clients.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is SEO still relevant with AI search tools?

Yes — being well-structured and authoritative matters even more now for both search engines and AI answer engines.

How long until I see ranking results?

In the course you'll learn realistic timelines and how to set client expectations, typically 3–6 months for competitive terms.

Do I need to know coding for technical SEO?

Basic HTML familiarity helps, but we teach exactly what you need — no full dev background required.

Can I offer SEO as a freelance service after?

Yes, most graduates start with local business audits as their first paid SEO clients.
