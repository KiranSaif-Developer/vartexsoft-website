Organic Growth to Paid Reach

Social Media Marketing Course

Learn to grow organic reach and run targeted ad campaigns while managing brand presence across Meta, LinkedIn, and TikTok.

You'll manage a real content and ad calendar end-to-end, the same way an in-house social media manager would for a growing brand.

What You'll Learn

  • Platform Strategy — tailoring content and tone for Meta, LinkedIn, and TikTok audiences.
  • Content Calendars — planning and batching content so brands post consistently.
  • Community Management — engagement tactics that turn followers into an active audience.
  • Paid Social Campaigns — running targeted ads across Meta and LinkedIn.
  • Analytics & Reporting — tracking engagement, reach, and campaign performance.
  • Brand Voice & Positioning — keeping every post consistent with brand identity.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to manage multiple platforms at once?

The course covers Meta, LinkedIn, and TikTok, but you can specialize based on your goals.

Is this course focused on organic or paid growth?

Both — you'll learn organic content strategy alongside running paid ad campaigns.

Can I manage social media as a freelance service after?

Yes, most graduates go on to manage social accounts for small businesses as freelancers or in-house.

How much content will I actually produce?

You'll plan and build a full month-long content calendar as part of the course.
