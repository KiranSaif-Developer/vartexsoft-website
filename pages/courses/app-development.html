One Codebase, Every Platform

App Development Course

Learn to build real, cross-platform mobile apps for Android and iOS using Flutter — one codebase, native performance.

You'll go from your first widget to a fully functional, published app, learning the same patterns used in production mobile apps.

What You'll Learn

  • Dart & Flutter Fundamentals — widgets, layouts, and app architecture from the ground up.
  • State Management — handling app data cleanly as your app grows in complexity.
  • APIs & Local Storage — connecting your app to backends and storing data on-device.
  • UI/UX for Mobile — building interfaces that feel native on both Android and iOS.
  • Testing & Debugging — catching bugs before your users do.
  • App Store Deployment — publishing your app to the Play Store and App Store.

How The Course Works

1

Flutter Foundations

Learn Dart syntax and core Flutter widgets by building small daily apps.

2

Guided App Build

Build a full-featured app with navigation, state, and API integration under mentor review.

3

Polish & Testing

Refine UI/UX, fix bugs, and prepare your app for real users.

4

Publish Your App

Package and submit your app to the Play Store (and App Store where applicable).

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need prior programming experience?

Basic logic/programming familiarity helps, but Dart is taught from scratch.

Will my app work on both Android and iOS?

Yes — Flutter lets you build for both platforms from a single codebase.

Will I actually publish an app?

Yes, the course walks you through submitting to the Play Store.

Can I build a business idea I already have?

Definitely — many students use their capstone project as the MVP for their own app idea.
