Shopify Course

Learn to design, set up, and launch fully optimized Shopify stores — for your own brand or as a freelance service for dropshipping and e-commerce clients.

You'll build a real, functioning store from scratch, covering everything from theme customization to checkout and post-launch optimization.

What You'll Learn

  • Store Setup & Theme Customization — building a professional storefront without needing to code.
  • Product & Catalog Management — organizing products, variants, and collections that convert.
  • Payments & Shipping Configuration — setting up checkout, taxes, and shipping the right way.
  • Apps & Conversion Tools — using Shopify apps for upsells, reviews, and abandoned cart recovery.
  • Store Speed & SEO — optimizing stores to load fast and rank in search.
  • Client Store Delivery — structuring stores for dropshipping brands or freelance clients.

Do I need my own product to take this course?

No, most customization is done through themes and apps, though basic edits are covered too.

Can I offer Shopify setup as a freelance service?

Yes — Shopify store setup is one of the most in-demand freelance services right now.

Does the course cover marketing the store too?

The focus is on building and optimizing the store; our Digital Marketing course pairs well for driving traffic.
