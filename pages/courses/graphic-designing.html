Design That Sells

Graphic Designing Course

Master Photoshop, Illustrator, and Canva to design brand identities, social posts, and marketing assets that businesses actually pay for.

You'll build a real design portfolio while learning the design principles agencies use to create work that looks intentional, not templated.

What You'll Learn

  • Design Fundamentals — color theory, typography, and layout principles that make designs work.
  • Photoshop for Marketing — photo editing, compositing, and ad creative design.
  • Illustrator & Branding — logo design, brand identity systems, and vector graphics.
  • Social Media Design — Canva workflows for fast, consistent, on-brand content.
  • Client Brief Interpretation — translating vague client requests into strong design direction.
  • Portfolio Building — packaging your work to attract paying clients.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need artistic talent to start?

No — design is a learnable skill built on principles, not raw talent.

Which software will I need?

Photoshop, Illustrator, and Canva — we cover setup and alternatives if budget is a concern.

Can I freelance with just Canva skills?

You can start there, but Photoshop and Illustrator open up higher-paying client work.

Will I have a portfolio by the end?

Yes — the whole course is structured to leave you with portfolio-ready projects.
