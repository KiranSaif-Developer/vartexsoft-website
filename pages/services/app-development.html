Frequently Asked Questions

Native or cross-platform — which is better?

Cross-platform (like Flutter) suits most business apps and saves cost; native makes sense for performance-heavy apps like games or AR. We'll recommend based on your case.

Do you handle App Store/Play Store submission?

Yes, including developer account setup guidance, store listing assets, and the review process.

What happens after the app is live?

We offer maintenance plans covering bug fixes, OS updates, and minor feature work.
